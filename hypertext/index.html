ESCAPISM.html

By ATROSED

2026/03/10




After I’ve discovered the opportunity to treat the web like my life journal, I sought to create a hyperlink art piece similar to Anthropy’s Queers in Love At The End Of The World and dlsan’s HyperMacbeth. Hypertext has the opportunity to become a narrative sequence that can be more than just a formal device. Although I was limited to code, I took the creative liberty to use this technique and reimagined it to reflect raw emotion through digital storytelling in HTML coding. This idea of narrating a sequence through poetic code gave me the freedom to take a moment and think about what I want my life to be—whimsical and full of grand possibilities that bring me to a state of zen. This reimagined world is an escapism from reality. By detaching myself to enter the digital world, I feel a sense of serenity. But the longer I stay, the more doomed I become. Eventually reality will sink in again and crush the safehaven I've built. I beg not to leave this space but I will have to. It’s my responsibility. But how long can I keep up with my responsibilities until my labored body tears down?
