LEARN PARAPARA WITH ME NOW!!!

I know exactly what you're thinking. Ananah, what is ParaPara and why do you like to ParaPara? Well, similar to most alternative styles,gyaruhas a dance style that almost all gyaru's do as a hobby. The dance sequence consists of mainly leg and arm movement, which sounds easy at first. But, when you're dancing to EuroBeat music with extremely fast tempos, the hands and arm movements can get quite complicated (don't worry, sometimes I have trouble, too).

That's why a lot of gyaru's practice this dance DAILY. Anytime, anywhere. Sometimes they will do it with friends or by themselves. But ParaPara's tend to shine a lot in a big syncronized group (mainly because it looks awesome seeing a group of people dance together). I'm gonna let you guys in a bit of a secret but...I only know simple techpara dances(i.e., Cyber Dance by Go Go Girls). A parapara dance I want to master the most before the end of this year however is CRAZY TRAIN (the third video on the right). The reason why is because I rock with its beat the most and I think I would have a lot of fun not only learning but also doing it once I get the hang of the hand movements. So, I invite you all to my space learn with me so we can all DANCE TOGETHER!!!! <3
