UNDERNEATH_MY_SKIN.jpeg

UNDERNEATH_MY_SKIN.jpeg is a glitched image of my persona's transformation made by using raw data of several images of myself from Adobe Photoshop and transforming it through Audacity before overlaying it all together. This persona is yet to have a name, but as it continues to expand and blossom, I grow more vulnerable. All of my flaws have unexpectedly grown into one being that cannot be contained by the shell of my perfectionism. This new self is beyond the layers of my skin, muscle, and bones. This self will only be bound by the digital world-the only surface on which I can express myself. It is a visual transformation beyond my physical anatomy; it is a rebirth of a new spirit with all of my flaws merged into one blossoming from my previous soul. It is made without any expectations and by exploiting unexpected glitches in media, it weaves itself in as a symbol of my flaws. Flaws that should celebrated and portrayed with integrity and beauty.

"UNDERNEATH_MY_SKIN.jpeg", 09/08/2026. 1179" x 1545"

POPPI-CHAN

Poppi-chan is a fictional character made up of symbolic images that portrays the kind of addictive media within the otaku subculture all over the world. To briefly explain the process, Poppi-Chan was created by various images saved from the internet and the core symbol of my topic was a 16 year old girl named Elizabeth from Seven Deadly Sins. All of these images that were pulled from the internet was then edited through Adobe Photoshop, finalizing the mashup. Instead of looking to real people to experience real emotions, most otakus look to the digital world to isolate themselves and indulge in their own fantasies like a drug. The only thing that makes them feel understood within their digital escapism is young girls; no matter what age or what they look like, young girls like Poppi-chan are only seen for their bodies because of their waifu material.

"Poppi-Chan", 09/17/2026. 72" x 72"

ROSEs REVERIE

Rose's Reverie is a fantastical adobe premiere video that consists of image framing and video clips that emphasizes what goes through the mind of Rose (a.k.a., myself reflected as a persona). Some of the clips presentend within this short film was coded through Google Colab as well. I am often easily distracted by the smallest things. Certain sounds and vibrant visuals are one of the main things that stimulate my brain until I am immersed within my own imagination. Even when I close my eyes, I remember these visuals and replay a distant memory that transforms into a misty dream. This piece is an abstract expression of my mind and the kinds of dreams that shape my thoughts into art through digital media. Every video clip, every transition, ever image and its framing are all purposefully random. My maladaptive dreams, daydreams and dreams are all sudden with no destination or goal. There is no clear sequence or meaning behind these symbols. However, it doesn’t mean they are nothing to me. These dreams are what help me think, and they help me consider what kind of approach I should try when creating an ambitious piece. It is a course of planning by regurgitating what I had learned through the day and adjusting those pieces I took to transform it into something the represents my mind personally. All of which is an expression that keeps my mind thinking about complex topics by taking pieces of my brain and experimenting with images to turn a thought into digital reality. This video is a psychological exploration of the enigma within my mind for viewers to freely interpret and question me. Although there is no right answer for this expression, nor a reason behind it, interpretations are the most astonishing through wonder.

"Rose's Reverie", 09/22/2026. 500" x 370"

ESCAPISM.html

Escapism.html was built off of the TextEdit app and supported by GitHub to allow public access to this project. As viewers begin the sequence, they will be met with a title page to start a poetic narrative discussing the need to abandon reality and embrace digital vortex (a false paradise). Each hyperlink will begin to build the digital realm suitable for the user as it constantly changes as you proceed through the poems. Although the sequence seems linear, there is more to Escapism beyond what the viewer thinks. This idea of narrating a sequence through poetic code gave me the freedom to take a moment and think about what I want my life to be—whimsical and full of grand possibilities that bring me to a state of zen. This reimagined world is an escapism from reality. By detaching myself to enter the digital world, I feel a sense of serenity. But the longer I stay, the more doomed I become. Eventually reality will sink in again and crush the safehaven I've built. I beg not to leave this space but I will have to. It’s my responsibility. But how long can I keep up with my responsibilities until my labored body tears down?

"Escapism.html", 10/06/2026.
