Little Miss Fuchsia

This thought dungeon pathway is quite recent. As I was taking pictures for my photography class, I managed to find my favorite ideal settings for my DSLR camera. After lowering the shutter speed a bunch, I was able to capture this fluid image. Because of the sunlight, I was able to manipulate the image to make it appear as if it's glowing. No edits were mad eon photoshop either. This is a RAW image of Little Miss Fuchsia. That's the vessel's name.

If you're wondering why I call them vessels is because I like to think that I'm using the lens to reveal something that is "invisible" to the naked eye. And by capturing living things under the lens by using a painterly technique, I was able to capture Little Miss Fuchsia's character-playful, whimsical, and rambunctious. With these character traits identified, we can determine that this vessel is alive and using the breeze to uplift them naturally so they can flow in the wind.
