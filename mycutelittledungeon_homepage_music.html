The reason why ATARASHII GAKKO's uniform's are school girls is because, in Japan, a lot of girl groups wear fancy and cute outfits. Often times, these outfits are revealing so that they cater to their majority fanbase (which would be mostly men). Because of this, Japanese idols are often sexualized and objectified. So, to protest against this, ATARASHII GAKKO decides to wear formal school girl uniforms to express comfortability and empowerment among youthful girls.
