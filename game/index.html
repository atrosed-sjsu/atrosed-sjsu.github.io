Put Me Back Together

By ATROSED

11/08/2026


Put Me Back Together is a mini horror game masked behind the innocense of a quest where the player is supposed to collect certain items the mannequin needs in order to be brought to life. Behind the creation of the mini-game, I utilized the ball catch game as a template and modified the rules and dynamics of the game. Instead of catching the ball, the player is collecting the items. As each level passes, the more the items will move faster. To increase the difficulty, I want the player to feel rushed by setting up a timer. So the player must get the task done at a certain period of time or else it's game over. There are even chances the target may escape so the player must proceed as fast as possible. The reason behind this mechanic is because as each level progresses, the more reality begins to sink in for the player. The player in this case is the admirer, obssessed with the idea of having a partner as their trophy. In this case, the time crunch is necessary because the admirer fears of being caught by the authorities. And as the targets become more disturbingly real, it will make the player question if they want to keep going for the sole purpose of having someone beside them. And even if the player so chooses to complete all of the tasks, their expectations will be inevitibly beaten by reality as this person speaking to them was never real.

As said previously, Put me Back Together is about an admirer, the player, that wants to find a partner to keep with them at all times. Eventually, the admirer finds someone called Bell. but they look quite strange. Instead of a complete person they seem incomplete and they wish to become human again. Swayed by their pleads, the admirer decides to help Bell in their quest to beome whole again. Even if the tasks seem daunting, she promises that it will go by fast and will reward the player with its beauty. Influenced by this incentive, the admirer sets out to complete her tasks in hopes of achieving this award. However, as the admirer continues to indulge into this fantasy, the harder they begin to fall towards reality as these tasks come with a cost. The reason why I decided to chose this subject for my game is because I wanted to touch on this idea of obsession and test how far a player is willing to go to complete a game. I want to test people's morals to see what they would do in order to complete their desires. After all, we all have slefish desires, goals, needs, and wants. But I will ask this this: How far are you willing to take it?
